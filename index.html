Closed beta

Try Hue-istic before launch

Use the same Google account for the group, the testing page, and Google Play on your Android phone. Follow these steps in order.

  1. Join the tester group

    Open Hue-istic Beta Testers and choose to join the group. You need to be a member before Google Play will let you join the test.

    Join the Google Group

  2. Become a tester on Google Play

    Open the testing page and select “Become a tester”. Joining the group alone does not opt you into the beta.

    Join the Google Play test

  3. Install and give it a try

    Open the store listing on your Android phone, install Hue-istic, and try it as your home screen launcher.

    Install from Google Play

Can’t access the test? Check that all three steps use the same Google account. If you just joined or the test was just published, allow time for Google Play to update and try again. If you’re already in the internal test, leave it before joining this closed beta.

Found a bug or have feedback? Email the developer with your phone model and what happened.
